Privacy Policy

Rclone Zorin DLNA is a personal application used to access Google Drive files with read-only permissions.

Google Drive authentication credentials are stored locally on the user's computer.

Selected media files may be streamed to devices on the user's local home network using DLNA.

Google Drive data is not sold or shared with advertisers. No Google Drive files or OAuth credentials are stored on this website.

Google Drive access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
